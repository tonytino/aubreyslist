The "Gluten-friendly" state is gone: celiac-safe is now the only community safety claim. Every listing on Aubrey's List is assumed to have gluten-free options, so the site no longer badges listings as merely gluten-friendly — a disputed or unattested celiac-safe claim simply shows no safety badge. The Gluten-friendly quick filter, amber badge, wheat-strike icon, and "Not yet attested" chip are removed; confirm/dispute votes and counts are unchanged.
