- `[manual]` The directory's prebuilt quick filters are now faceted multi-select (`?quick=` is a comma-set): the safety pair (Celiac-safe / Gluten-friendly) is mutually exclusive, while Recently verified is an additive toggle, and selections AND-compose server-side (e.g. celiac-safe places that were recently verified with no recent incident). Picking a safety chip replaces its sibling; recency combines with either. The group model is built so a future additive "Favorited" filter slots in.
