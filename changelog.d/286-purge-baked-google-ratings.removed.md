- `[manual]` Stopped capturing Google star ratings into the baked seed file, and stripped the 280 `googleRating`/`googleRatingCount` keys from the current `scripts/seed-listings.generated.json` — prior values remain in git history, unchanged. ADR-014 forbids persisting Google ratings "not to the DB, not to blob storage, not to committed JSON (seeds, fixtures)". GMP ToS §3.2.3(b) permits caching only what the Service Specific Terms expressly allow. The Service Specific Terms record two express carve-outs — Google ID values (§A.3) and lat/lng for 30 days (§B.14.3) — and `rating` appears in neither. Dropping `places.rating`/`places.userRatingCount` from the Text Search field mask also stops every seed refresh from eating into the scarcer Enterprise-tier free quota (1k/month) instead of Pro's (5k) — Google prices a call by its highest-tier field. No money was ever at stake either way: a manually-dispatched refresh runs ~140 calls, well under both caps. Nothing consumed the values: `seed.ts` never read them and the browse loader never populated the card view-model field.
