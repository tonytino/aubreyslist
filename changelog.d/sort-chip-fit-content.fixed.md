- `[manual]` The sort and radius chips are now as wide as the option you picked, not as wide as the longest one. A native `<select>` takes its intrinsic width from its widest `<option>`, so "Near me" sat in a chip stretched to fit "Alphabetical (A–Z)". Both controls now share a grid cell with an invisible sizer span carrying the selected label, and the `<select>` gets `min-w-0` so it fills that width instead of forcing it. No change to the markup's semantics: still one labelled native `<select>`, and the sizer is `aria-hidden` and `invisible` (never `hidden`, which would collapse the cell it exists to size).
